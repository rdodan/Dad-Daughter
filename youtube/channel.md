# Channel: Dad, how does it work?

Channel picture: `youtube/channel-picture.png` (800×800; YouTube shows it as a circle).

## Description

I started this channel for my daughter. She asks "but how does it work?" about everything: the Wi-Fi, the fridge, the plane in the sky. I wanted a way for us to learn new things together that's fun, curious and a little bit interactive.

So every episode, Dad takes one thing we use every day and explains it in the simplest way possible, with stick figures, a lamp, a flashlight, whatever it takes. The daughter in the cartoon asks the questions you're secretly wondering too, makes the wrong guesses we all make, and explains it back at the end.

It's for curious kids and for every grown-up who has nodded along without really knowing how something works.

Got a question you want Dad to explain? Leave it in the comments. The best ones become the next episode.

New episodes regularly. Subscribe and learn something new with us.
