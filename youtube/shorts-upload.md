Why does Wi-Fi get stuck in your room? #shorts

### Description

Your Wi-Fi isn't broken. Your walls are eating it. 🧱📶

Wi-Fi is a kind of light your eyes can't see. Every wall soaks some of it up, so the further your room is from the router, the weaker it gets, until your tablet misses some "blinks" and has to ask again. That's the spinning circle.
