Dad & daughter: three ways to draw them

All three are plain stick figures on white. They differ in how each character is told apart at thumbnail size, and in how much drawing each frame takes.

A. Line only

Glasses for him, pigtails for her, nothing else. Fastest to draw and animate. The two can blur together when the frame is small.

B. One colour each My pick

Still stick figures, but he owns blue and she owns orange. You can tell who is who at a glance, and the same two colours can mark who is speaking in captions.

C. Marker doodle

Thick, wobbly lines as if drawn on a whiteboard. The most charm and the most personality, but every pose has to be redrawn by hand to keep the wobble.

Her four faces

The daughter carries the reactions, so most episodes need only these four heads swapped onto the same body.
