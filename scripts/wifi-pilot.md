# Pilot: How does Wi-Fi work?

Target length: about 3 minutes 30 seconds. Lines marked **(to viewer)** are said straight to the camera.

## Cold open (0:00 – 0:30)

*Daughter alone, holding a tablet. A loading circle spins on it.*

**DAUGHTER:** Ugh! It's stuck AGAIN! Dad! The Wi-Fi is broken!

*Dad walks in.*

**DAD:** It's not broken. You walked too far from the router.

**DAUGHTER:** The what?

*The router is drawn in, lights blinking.*

**DAD:** That little box with the blinking lights.

**DAUGHTER (to viewer):** Be honest. Do YOU know how a cartoon gets from that box into my tablet? …Me neither.

**DAUGHTER:** There's no wire, Dad. How does it get in?

**DAD:** It flies.

**DAUGHTER:** It FLIES?!

*Title: How does Wi-Fi work?*

## Invisible light (0:30 – 1:15)

**DAD:** Yep. Your cartoon flies across the room. You just can't see it.

**DAUGHTER:** Like a ghost?

**DAD:** Like light. You know how a lamp shines in every direction?

**DAUGHTER:** Yeah.

*Blue rings spread out from the router.*

**DAD:** The router is a lamp. It shines all the time, in every direction. But it shines a kind of light your eyes can't see.

**DAUGHTER:** Invisible light? You made that up.

**DAD:** It's real. It's called radio waves. It's the same stuff that brings music to the car radio.

**DAUGHTER (to viewer):** Hang on. If it's just a lamp, how is a lamp sending me a cartoon?

*A flashlight blinks: on, off, on, on, off.*

**DAD:** Ever seen someone blink a flashlight to send a message? On, off, on, on, off.

**DAUGHTER:** Like a secret code!

**DAD:** Exactly. The router blinks its invisible light in a code. Just really, really fast. Millions of blinks every second.

**DAUGHTER:** MILLIONS?

**DAD:** And your tablet has a tiny part inside that can see that light. It reads the blinks and turns them back into a picture.

**DAUGHTER:** So my cartoon is… blinks.

**DAD:** Your cartoon is a LOT of blinks.

## Where the cartoon lives (1:15 – 1:55)

**DAUGHTER (to viewer):** But wait. Where does the box get the cartoon from? Is it inside the box?

*A cable runs from the router into the wall, under a street, under the sea, to a big computer.*

**DAD:** Nope. See this cable in the back? It goes into the wall, under the street, sometimes under the whole ocean, to a giant computer far away. That's where the cartoon lives.

**DAUGHTER:** So when I press play…

**DAD:** Your tablet blinks a message to the router: "One cartoon, please." The router passes it down the cable. The big computer sends the cartoon back, chopped into tiny pieces. And the router blinks those pieces over to you.

**DAUGHTER:** All that when I press one button?

**DAD:** In less time than it takes you to blink.

## Why it gets stuck (1:55 – 2:25)

**DAUGHTER:** Then why did it get stuck in my room?

**DAD:** What happens to a lamp's light when you close the door?

**DAUGHTER:** It gets dark.

*The blue rings fade as they pass through two walls.*

**DAD:** Same thing. Walls soak up some of the invisible light. More walls, weaker light. Your tablet missed some blinks, so it had to ask again. That's the spinning circle.

**DAUGHTER (to viewer):** So THAT'S why it works better near the box. Did you know that?

## Two quick ones (2:25 – 3:00)

**DAUGHTER:** And why is it slow when you and Mom are on it too?

**DAD:** The router has to share its blinks between all of us. More people, less for each.

**DAUGHTER:** So it's your fault.

**DAD:** …Sometimes.

**DAUGHTER:** Last one. Why does it need a password?

*The rings pass through the wall into the neighbour's house.*

**DAD:** The light goes through our walls into the neighbors' house too. The password scrambles our blinks so only our gadgets can read them.

**DAUGHTER:** So nobody can watch my cartoons.

**DAD:** Nobody wants to watch your cartoons.

## She explains it back (3:00 – 3:30)

**DAD:** Okay. Your turn. How does Wi-Fi work?

**DAUGHTER:** The box is a lamp with invisible light. It blinks really fast in a secret code. My tablet reads the blinks. And walls eat the light.

**DAD:** That's… actually right.

**DAUGHTER (to viewer):** Now you know too. What should Dad explain next? Tell us in the comments.

**DAUGHTER:** Dad, what's inside the ocean cable?

**DAD:** Next time.
